Lag Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Lag Bot ("the game", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is published on Google Play by its developer (contact details below).

1. Information on your phone

Your progress (stars, coins, items, settings) is saved on your phone. It is not sent to us. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy of this save is also stored in your Play Games account (see section 5) so you can move it to a new phone.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional "rewarded" ads you choose to watch (for double coins, a revive or an extra gift) and, after the first world, occasional full-screen ads between levels. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information and ad interactions to show, measure and (where you allow it) personalise ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is loaded. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. The Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads between levels.

Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To make the game better we use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They collect game events (for example: a level started, won or lost and which obstacle caused a hit, tutorial steps, boosters and ads used, purchases made), an app-instance ID, device model, operating system version, country and crash details. This information is not used to identify you. We also use Firebase Remote Config to adjust game settings (such as prices or ad frequency) without an update.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases (Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and gem packs) are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The game receives only which product you bought, so it can give it to you and restore it later.

5. Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, the game uses your Play Games account to store your save in the cloud and to post your Signal Run distance to a public weekly leaderboard under your Play Games name. Sign-in is optional; the game works fully without it.

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Children

Lag Bot is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will help remove any related data.

8. Keeping and deleting data

Uninstalling the game deletes the save on your phone. Your cloud save can be deleted from your Google Play Games account settings. Analytics data is kept by Firebase for up to 14 months. To ask about or delete data linked to your app-instance ID, contact us.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object to or limit its use. Contact us to use these rights.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
