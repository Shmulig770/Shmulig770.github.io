Beam Bender privacy policy

S Level Games · last updated 3 November 2026

What the game keeps

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars and settings) is saved only on your device. We don't run our own servers and don't collect your name, email or location.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and basic device information to show ads and measure them. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Remove Ads is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we only receive a confirmation that you bought it.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.
