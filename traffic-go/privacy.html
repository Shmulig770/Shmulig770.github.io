Traffic Go Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

This policy explains what information the Traffic Go game ("the app") by S Level Games ("we") uses and why.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (stars, unlocked worlds and levels, coins, power-ups and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not have user accounts and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

The app can show rewarded ads from Google AdMob, which you choose to watch (for example to continue a level after a crash). To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Remove Ads and coin packs) are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. The store tells the app only which product was bought, so it can be delivered and restored.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Information is shared only with the services named above (Google AdMob, Google Play, Apple App Store) so that they can provide ads and purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
